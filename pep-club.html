Club Description

The purpose of the South Iron Pep Club is to promote and develop school spirit, as well as set an example of good behavior, positive attitudes and good sportsmanship at athletic events. We will encourage leadership qualities and life-long social skills.

Membership Information

Membership Dues: $20

Students recieve a FREE Pep Club T-shirt (This shirt should be worn to get into the season HOME sporting events for free.)

Rules And Conduct

Students should wear there pep club shirt or South Iron apparel to get in free

Members should attend atleast 2 home games per athletic sason

Members should sit together in the designate Pep Club area and remain engaged in the game

Members are expected to maintain a postive attitude and good sportsmanship

SHOW SCHOOL SPIRIT!

Be respectful!

Students should remain in the gym unless using the restroom or making a purchase from concession stand

All school rules and policies still apply at home or away games

ALL members are expected to participate in all club events. If you do not participate you will not get out of class for planning or decorating.

Consequences

The sponsors and administrators have the authority to excuse any member from the Pep Club
